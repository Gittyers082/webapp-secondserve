# Hapag brand

**Hapag** is Tagalog for the dining table. Tagline: *A place at the table for everyone.*

## The mark

The h of "hapag" is drawn as a table, with a bowl set on top of it: a meal set out
for whoever comes to the table. It is built on a 256-unit grid from 36-unit strokes
with fully rounded ends; the bowl is a half circle of radius 41.

## Wordmark

"hapag", lowercase, set in Instrument Serif at about 1.2 times the cap height of
the text beside it. In the app the lockup is the tile followed by the wordmark
(`src/components/Logo.astro`).

## Using it

- Keep clear space around the mark at least as wide as one stroke (36 / 256 of its height).
- Smallest size: 16 px for the tile. Below 24 px the bowl merges into the table,
  but the h still reads.
- Put the tile on any background. Put the bare symbol on light backgrounds only.
- Do not recolour the bowl, outline the mark, add shadows, stretch it, or set
  "hapag" in another typeface.
- The name sits close to Hapag-Lloyd, a shipping company. Run a trademark search
  before any public launch.
